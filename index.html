Curious about All Nations Youth? Start by connecting with the church. The team can introduce you to the youth community and help you take your next step.

We meet on Saturdays from 4–6 pm, Halifax local time. For the meeting location and any schedule updates, visit @any.hfx on Instagram or contact the church before your first visit.
